# 🎯 Day 1 – Simple Number Problems

## 🧩 Core Concept Focus
- Loops (`for`, `while`)
- Simple arithmetic operations
- Step-by-step thinking (input → logic → output)

---

## 🧠 Questions List

### 1. Print Numbers from 1 to N
**Input:**  
`5`  
**Output:**  
`1 2 3 4 5`  

✨ Teaches basic loop construction.

---

### 2. Print Numbers from N to 1 (without changing loop condition)
**Input:**  
`5`  
**Output:**  
`5 4 3 2 1`  

✨ Encourages creative thinking and logic manipulation inside the loop.

---

### 3. Print All Even Numbers from 1 to N
**Input:**  
`10`  
**Output:**  
`2 4 6 8 10`  

✨ Introduces conditional checks inside loops.

---

### 4. Sum of First N Natural Numbers
**Input:**  
`5`  
**Output:**  
`15`  

✨ Encourages cumulative addition and variable usage.

---

### 5. Product (Factorial) of N
**Input:**  
`5`  
**Output:**  
`120`  

✨ Reinforces loop control with multiplication logic.

---

### 6. Sum of All Even Numbers up to N
**Input:**  
`10`  
**Output:**  
`30 (2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10)`  

✨ Combines loops + conditions + accumulation.

---

### 7. Print Squares of Numbers from 1 to N
**Input:**  
`5`  
**Output:**  
`1 4 9 16 25`  

✨ Introduces arithmetic pattern generation.

---

# 🏠 Homework / Practice Challenge

### 8. Print all numbers divisible by 3 and 5 up to N
**Input:**  
`30`  
**Output:**  
`15 30`  

✨ Reinforces conditional checks inside loops.

---

### 9. Find the sum of all odd numbers up to N
**Input:**  
`10`  
**Output:**  
`25 (1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9)`  

✨ Combines looping with conditional accumulation.

---

### 10. Print the cubes of numbers from 1 to N
**Input:**  
`5`  
**Output:**  
`1 8 27 64 125`  

✨ Extends arithmetic patterns beyond squares.

---

### 11. Print only numbers that are both even and perfect squares
**Input:**  
`1 to 20`  
**Output:**  
`4 16`  

✨ Encourages combining multiple conditions in a single loop.
